Backstage · Teoria do Delito ↗ · as of 14/10 · main 930743d

The oldest course, and the most hedged prose on the site.

A 1 Everglades course: started 08/09 as the site's first build, rewritten as split units on 27–28/09. 15 units, each in two parts. It never entered the pipeline: no shelf, no triage, no plan, no house layer.

What went well

  • Every unit tests before it explains. Each part opens on a decision question (A fired every shot, then drove B to hospital: which figure of art. 15?), and the answers give the reason.
  • The doctrine is on the page. Tavares, Roxin, Hungria, Sanches, Lang-Hinrichsen, set against the Brazilian article each time.
  • Unit 08's second part is the best. Interrupção e resposta: a five-way classification form, 3,165 words, negated inference 1.6.

What went badly

  • Negated inference is ten times the limit. Median 5.5 per 1,000 words, 7.4 on 11-reprovação; the worst course on the site by double. 'A questão não é…, mas…' is the voice.
  • Cases are letters. A, B, G the doctor: hypotheticals stand where decided cases should be.
  • No source block on any of the 32 pages. The authors are summarised, never quoted.
  • An orphan page. unidade-08-original (5,921 words, 10/09) is still in the tree and linked from nowhere.
  • Plain bold everywhere. Up to 88 per page (15).

Next, in order

  1. Deslop negated inference by span, course-wide, measured; it is the one fault that touches every page.
  2. Then the line from S0: the professor's own Lições Introdutórias is on the shelf list, and the exams give the bank.
  3. Replace lettered hypotheticals with decided cases where the sources have them; keep the opening questions.
  4. Remove unidade-08-original.

Thoughts

Delito was the experiment that started the site, and it still shows the habit that the slop measurements later named: every claim arrives as a denial of a wrong one. That habit is cheap to fix by span and should be fixed first, because the structure underneath is good: questions before explanation, doctrine set against the statute. The course needs real cases and real quotations more than it needs new pages.
