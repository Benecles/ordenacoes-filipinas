Short, clear lessons that never got the books prepared for them.

A 1 Everglades course. Written 24/09 with animated figures; pipeline v1 then built its shelf, triage and 17 compendia (30/09–03/10), and no lesson was rewritten from them. Only Aula 01 was rebuilt, on 30/09.

What went well

  • The lessons are clear. Aula 11 does lesão vs cláusula abusiva in 873 words with a real TJDFT appeal (35 shows for a website domain) and five sharp test questions.
  • The shelf is the biggest on the site. 452 sources, 1,775 triage rows, 17 compendia.
  • Aula 13 shows the depth. 3,326 words on the base do negócio, reading Luis Renato Ferreira da Silva against the CDC.
  • Low tic. Negated inference median 1.1/1k, the lowest after Controle.

What went badly

  • A third of a sitting. Median 1,107 words; 16 of 17 lessons under 2,000.
  • The prepared sources were never used. Compendia since 03/10; no plan, no rewrite.
  • No source on the page. 0 source blocks, no house layer.
  • Old books for a changed code. Venosa (2016) and Caio Mário (2014) predate Lei 13.874/2019; slides missing for Aulas 15–17.

Next, in order

  1. Through the line from S0b: the exam bank first, then a course blueprint over the existing compendia.
  2. Deepen rather than replace: most pages are correct and short; a rebuild keeps their cases and tests.
  3. Route post-2019 arts. 421/421-A through Gerson Branco's articles, as noted on 30/09.
  4. Ask the chairman for the slides of Aulas 15–17.

Thoughts

Contratos is Controle's twin with better writing. Its pages are short because they were written from slides in one day; they are good because the writer stayed on one case and asked real questions. The work of finding the sources is done and sitting unused. This is the cheapest course to bring to the house bar: deepen each lesson from its compendium, keep what it already does well.
