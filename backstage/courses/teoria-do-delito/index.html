Teoria do Delito

Teoria geral do crime, tentativa e concurso de pessoas.

The oldest course, and the most hedged prose on the site.

Went well

  • Every unit tests before it explains.Each part opens on a decision question (A fired every shot, then drove B to hospital: which figure of art. 15?), and the answers give the reason.
  • The doctrine is on the page.Tavares, Roxin, Hungria, Sanches, Lang-Hinrichsen, set against the Brazilian article each time.
  • Unit 08's second part is the best.Interrupção e resposta: a five-way classification form, 3,165 words, negated inference 1.6.

Went badly

  • Negated inference is ten times the limit.Median 5.5 per 1,000 words, 7.4 on 11-reprovação; the worst course on the site by double. 'A questão não é…, mas…' is the voice.
  • Cases are letters.A, B, G the doctor: hypotheticals stand where decided cases should be.
  • No source block on any of the 32 pages.The authors are summarised, never quoted.
  • An orphan page.unidade-08-original (5,921 words, 10/09) is still in the tree and linked from nowhere.
  • Plain bold everywhere.Up to 88 per page (15).
