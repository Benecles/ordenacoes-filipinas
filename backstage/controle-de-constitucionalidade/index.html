Backstage · Controle de Constitucionalidade ↗ · as of 14/10 · main 80e4c9d

Its drawings are now the house's; its text is still mostly slide notes.

A 1 Everglades course with a 2 Sequoia front. The first course on the site (23/09). Its shelf, triage and 36 compendia were built in pipeline v1, and five lessons were rewritten then; the other 30 are the drafts of 23–25/09, written from slides. No exam bank, no course plan, no foundry.

What went well

  • Every figure redrawn on 14/10. Aulas 03–36: 24 lessons' figures rebuilt from the house families (document, bench, dot field, tracks, timeline, form, score, frame, struck norms, stamps), plus hero strips for 30–36, which had none. Blue = decided and orange = limit throughout; the old code (blue = diffuse, orange = concentrated) is gone from the drawings.
  • The preparation is done. Shelf of 100 sources, 1,435 triage rows, a compendium for each of the 36 lessons (median 45,921 words after the S4 rebuild).
  • Aula 24 shows what the course could be. 9 source blocks, statute-anchored, 0 negated inference; it was already the decent one on 30/09.
  • Aula 10 carries the house layer best. 16 marks, 3 source blocks, colour with meaning across a two-lesson axis (preventive blue, repressive red).
  • The v1 rewrites doubled the pages. Aula 01 went from 940 to 3,211 words; 02, 04, 05 and 08 are 2,157–2,652.

What went badly

  • Half a sitting per lesson. Median 1,480 words against 2,974 site-wide; Aula 14 is 783 words, a nine-minute page.
  • Thirty of 36 lessons are the 25/09 drafts. Written from slides, before any source reached a writer.
  • Slides were the only source. Aulas 10–16 record their source as one slide deck each (13 to 33 pages), in a hidden title cell; 13 lessons carry hidden provenance notes. Readers don't see these, and they are the record of what the writer had.
  • Almost no sources on the page. 32 of 36 lessons have no source block; Aula 14 names ADI 2.995 and gives no holding.
  • Plans stalled. Six blueprints, one approved (Aula 03), none written from.
  • The house layer reached five lessons. Plain bold is still up to 78 per page (Aula 03).

Next, in order

  1. Make Controle the next course through 4 Grand Canyon after Latam. S0–S4 exist, so it starts at the exam bank and the course blueprint.
  2. Run the foundry on Aula 14 (bench: thinnest) and Aula 30 (holdout: the ADC pilot, never written).
  3. Cut what the pages say about missing cases (Aula 14 named ADI 2.995 and then excused its 'structural example'); that went live 14/10.

Thoughts

Controle is the clearest case of the line's order of operations. Its pages are thin because their writers had slides and nothing else; the books arrived in the compendia a week later and no writer ever opened them. The five v1 rewrites and the two good lessons show the material is there. What the course needs is to be written once, from the sources it already has, with a plan that says what each lesson adds. Until then, the cheap win is to stop the pages talking about their sources.
