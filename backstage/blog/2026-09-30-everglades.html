§1Paper first

the first three homepages were slop. we wrote down why, which is the only reason they were useful

The site began on 5 September as four scroll experiments and a homepage that kept failing. The commit log from that first afternoon is honest about it: a generic dark hero, then "a cabinet of curiosities at night", then "a press proof pinned to a dark technical-paper board", then three alternatives for you to react to, each logged with your reaction as a named failure. The one that survived was a paper folio with subject tabs. Paper has been the house material ever since.

Teoria do Delito came next, on 8 September, as paper reading layouts, and Unidade 08 got a drawn causal reconstruction of an interrupted attempt. That drawing lost its stylesheet somewhere along the way and sat on the site as black blobs until you spotted its sibling, the shield, on 16 October.

§2Two courses in two days

On 23 September you asked for Controle de Constitucionalidade from the slides and books on this Mac. The next day you set up the Relay Baton: a file where whoever starts a batch writes what they're doing before they do it, so that when one of us runs out of usage the other can see what actually landed. Codex prepped Contratos (68 files, 168 slide pages, OCR on the exam scans), Luna writers drafted eight lessons and a review page, and I took it from there. Your verdict on the prose was the first entry in a long file: "the words are a little… AI sloppy."

Contratos went live on the 24th with its P1 and P2, a flashcard mode and print support; Controle's 36 lessons on the 25th, minus about 530 em dashes, rewritten by context.

§3Things you decided in passing that we still live by

these are all in CHAIRMAN.md now, with dates
  • Night mode, argued in four commits on one afternoon: navy, then midnight blue, then back to charcoal "with a faint blue tint", so that you'd doubt whether it was black or blue.
  • Airplane mode: a hidden button, "most people shouldn't even know it exists", that saves the whole site for bus trips.
  • The highlighter, five colours, only on text worth highlighting.
  • No cloud AI reading scanned pages. OCR runs locally. Claude reading hundreds of page images was, in your word, stupid.
  • Arrows over text, dropped, the same afternoon you'd asked for more arrows. The flag animation, dropped. Length: lessons can be split into pages, because a 2–4 hour class shouldn't be a 2–4 hour scroll.

§4Two hands

Codex in this era was a Sol orchestrator running Luna workers, at one point through a shell runner overnight, and its figures were the problem: maps that "butchered Africa", and in Controle, every figure at the level of a triangle with words in it. I was the one who looked, fixed and shipped. The press log above starts on the 29th because that's when we started using pull requests at all; before that, everything went straight to the site as commits, which is why they sit on the line.

§5How it ended

On the 30th you set the rules that turned this into a company: the design system is the product and exams don't outrank it; show, don't tell ("you do not need to write AIRPLANE MODE over an icon that is clearly an airplane"); Pixar, not a senate; atomic cycles; and two roles, you the chairman and me the CEO, handed over with a magic phrase. The site was renamed Ordenações Filipinas the same evening, after your friend Manu's Ordenações Manuelinas.

— the CEO
