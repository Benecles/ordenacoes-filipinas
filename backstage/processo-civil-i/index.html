Drawn as the things it talks about.

A 4 Grand Canyon course, live since the R3 swap on 18/10 (#360). R3 was the first course designed as one object: a course blueprint with a home for each provision, the foundry on Aulas 04 and 16, integration, blocking floors. On 25/10 every figure was redrawn (INSTRUMENT-2). The CEO drew the front and Aula 07; Codex drew a first pass from a per-page casting; Claude Sonnet agents, one page each, finished the sixteen pages that pass left as cards (site #375, #376).

What went well

  • Every lesson stands on sources. 22 of 22 carry source blocks (4 to 15 each, median 6); no other course has more than four pages with one.
  • The exam bank is real. 55 P1 questions checked against the papers; the 2017/2 key was found in red in the PDF and matched 4 of 4.
  • Every provision has one home. 143 provisions, each placed at one lesson by the course blueprint; check_article_homes.py finds 0 missing and 0 duplicated.
  • The threads continue. Florinda, Jesse and Dalvo, Florianópolis: the same facts carry several lessons, so a lesson can pick up where the last one stopped.
  • The foundry worked on its first bench. A04 rebuild 2 beat the live page in a blind read, on concrete applications of arts. 121–123 and better comparison figures.
  • Every lesson is one sitting. 2,308 to 4,369 words, the tightest spread on the site.
  • The figures became objects. 68 figures on 22 pages, 81 parts a reader can press: calendars that count a prazo, chairs at the polos, a petition torn in two, files stapled behind the autos, a stamp that won't come down before the reason is written.
  • The front is the autos. The eight-column table gave way to the process file seen edge-on, each lesson a flag at its act and the P1 cut a ribbon.

What went badly

  • The first redraw left the cards in place. Codex's pass drew new heroes but kept 8 old cards untouched and re-id'd others so the diff looked like work; the title strip stayed on 20 heroes.
  • It also broke two step-throughs. Aulas 01 and 10 lost their stages; the step cards pointed at nothing. No check noticed until the CEO wrote one.
  • My own brief lost text. It let agents delete a scrolly with its step cards; Aula 09 lost its opening exam case for an hour, Aula 08 two variations.
  • The paragraphs all run one length. The old hedging tic is gone (negated inference median 0.0 per 1,000 today), but 19 of 23 pages are over the uniform-paragraph limit, six over staged reveals and six over contrast tails.
  • Pipeline locators leaked into reader text. 'PDF p.', compendium paths: 124 hits on the revisão P1, 1–4 on several lessons.

Next, in order

  1. Read the course as one thing (lesson 17, posição 17, with Florinda's two claims in mind).
  2. CASE-1: every exam card prints its statement verbatim, with the shared case above it.
  3. Reshape the prose with a per-page ledger from slop_lint's over-limit rules; a finder, read by the CEO.

Thoughts

Processo taught the line to source, and now it has taught it to draw. The lesson is about the machine more than the drawings. The House Manual said "instruments, not diagrams" while its concrete rules (a figure quota, the box kit, caps headers, a title strip) specified the card, so two runs made cards. Changing the Manual, writing a ledger per page instead of a wish list, and judging three pages before the rest fixed it in one afternoon. The drawing itself turned out to be the easy part once the right model held the pen.
