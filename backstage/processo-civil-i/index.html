The house's reference course, now live in its third build and waiting for the chairman's read.

A 4 Grand Canyon course, live since the swap on 18/10 (#360). R2 ran the whole of pipeline v1 and swapped on 09/10. R3 was the first course designed as one object: a course blueprint with a home for each provision, the foundry on Aulas 04 and 16, integration, blocking floors. Sixteen lessons were rebuilt in R3; six were kept from R2 and deslopped, so they still read 3 Yosemite. Since the swap: one sentence per figure on what the reader does with it (INSTRUMENT-1, 19/10), and the exam cards are next (CASE-1, #260).

What went well

  • Every lesson stands on sources. 22 of 22 carry source blocks (4 to 15 each, median 6); no other course has more than four pages with one.
  • The exam bank is real. 55 P1 questions checked against the papers; the 2017/2 key was found in red in the PDF and matched 4 of 4.
  • Every provision has one home. 143 provisions, each placed at one lesson by the course blueprint; check_article_homes.py finds 0 missing and 0 duplicated.
  • The threads continue. Florinda, Jesse and Dalvo, Florianópolis: the same facts carry several lessons, so a lesson can pick up where the last one stopped.
  • The foundry worked on its first bench. A04 rebuild 2 beat the live page in a blind read, on concrete applications of arts. 121–123 and better comparison figures.
  • Every lesson is one sitting. 2,308 to 4,369 words, the tightest spread on the site.

What went badly

  • The prose got worse while the sourcing got better. Negated inference median 2.5 per 1,000 words, five times the limit; A04 went from 2.1 to 4.5, A11 from 1.0 to 4.2. The gate only warned until 10/10.
  • The pages narrate what their sources lack. Gap talk at 5.5/1k on A15, 3.9 on A10 and A16, against 0.03 in human doctrine. Measured only from today.
  • Three gate faults shipped past green checks. The revisão P1 lost its hero, A14's captions numbered wrong, 31 QA screenshots in the public tree. All checks passed on all three.
  • Nobody designed R2 as one object. Parallel writers could not see each other; the course blueprint came only with R3.
  • A04's first carrier was wrong. SINDIFUMO, an atypical collective case, carried the normal rule until 14/10; Didier's sublocatário is the ordinary example. Fault at the course plan.
  • Pipeline locators leaked into reader text. 'PDF p.', compendium paths: 124 hits on the revisão P1, 1–4 on several lessons.

Next, in order

  1. Read the course as one thing (lesson 17, posição 17, with Florinda's two claims in mind) before anything else is built on it.
  2. CASE-1: every exam card prints its statement verbatim, with the shared case above it.
  3. Write the RETRO with one stage attribution per fault, so Olympia inherits the causes and not the symptoms.

Thoughts

Processo is where the line learned to work. It proved that preparation transfers: a verified bank, a shelf with real treatises, and every lesson came out sourced. It also proved that sourcing is not writing. The pages got longer, more careful and more hedged, and every one of my reads found something the checks passed. What changed the result was designing the course first and reading lessons blind against their predecessors. That is the process to carry forward, and Processo should stop being rebuilt once R3 lands.
