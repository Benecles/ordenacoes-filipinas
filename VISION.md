# VISION: Metodologia Jurídica, Aula 01

**What the reader carries away.** One Roman text stayed fixed while jurists wrapped it in notes, tied it to living local law, and finally lost it as the law actually applied. The method (gloss, relate, argue from reasons) outlived the book.

**Spine.** Hero = a planta, read left to right as time: three rails of sources (romano, canônico, direito próprio) run through Bolonha (a fixed nucleus gathers notes), the comentadores (cross-ties bind the rails into one consilium), and the sixteenth century (the national rail rises above, the Roman rail drops to subsidiary and splits into depuração and vigência). Then the five chapters in the book's order.

**Figures** (judged by the words-off test: if only arcs, stacks, wraps and positions remain, it still says something):
- Hero: redrawn as the planta above (the open book is gone).
- Consulta (A6-A7a): rethought, three shelves of volumes stacked in the order of consultation. Static plate.
- Volumes escolares (A8): rethought, five real bindings, the Volumen parvum opened into distinct quires. A9's band is cut.
- Glosa: universidade + folios merged into ONE folio in three states (notes, cross-references, apparatus around the same fixed nucleus). The B1-B4 open-book is cut.
- Parecer: statutes + consilium merged into three states (two cities; the road crossing the border; sources writing into one sheet).
- Terra: three states with identical geometry, no inner line.
- Vínculos (C6b-c): added, the map of two territories (land fixed, movables follow the person).
- Apresentação: rethought, one document, two routes to one door.
- Tópica plate: cut (arrows converging on a rectangle); the companion page keeps the topic.
- Autoridade: rethought, a balance: many volumes can rise above one contrary sheet.
- Crise: three tables of consultation, same books, changing positions.
- Depuração (E5) and vigência (E8-E9): added; they replace the generic ending plate.
