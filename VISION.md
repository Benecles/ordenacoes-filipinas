# VISION: Metodologia 01.2, A tópica e a dialética

**Carry away.** A commentator never leaves the statute: he writes round it, from several sides, and another hand writes against what he wrote. Tópica finds the relations; dialética exposes them to objection; the objection returns to the same line.

**Spine, book order.**
1. Hero = one relational planta, a manuscript folio: the statute's column stays whole; four marginal glosses (definir/dividir, causas, comparação, autoridades) tie to the same line; an interlinear extension ("pelo procurador") sits on that line; an objection in the other ink crosses the extension and returns to the line. Hover a gloss: its thread lights, the rest recede.
2. Ch.1 origins: prose as now; Baldo tally stays (the count is the picture).
3. Ch.2: the three paragraphs that build the tree become a stepped Porphyrian tree in place (donation under contract; contract under relação jurídica; division back down). New comparator for a pari / a fortiori beside T12. The rest stays prose.
4. Ch.3: the cord of leaves becomes a collation table: aligned excerpts of one line across commentaries, three states (one expert's credit; the repeated reading orienting; the counter-reading crossing it).
5. Ch.4: the eight round stations become one folio in three states (literal column; divisions and parallel excerpts; marginal notes and the objection), stepped with the three paragraphs that name the operations.

**Cut.** The old `percurso` book-and-scale, `conceitos` plate, `opinio` cord, `leitura` stations: words-off, each was labels on drawn books. No figure for T2, T3–T4, T10, T11, T13, T25–T27: nothing concrete to draw.

**Display.** No 480 px step floors; stepped drawings sit in place, centred, same grid cell. Text, quotes and exam items untouched.
