# VISION: Aula 01.2, Pedidos, causas e controle da inicial

**What the reader carries away.** The prestação owed shapes the pedido; the relation between pedidos shapes the order of judgment; interpreting, correcting and stabilizing the petition keep the same objects (a machine, a receipt, a coin) alive from petição to dispositivo.

**Spine.**
1. Hero: one planta, the petition of Cecília. Machine, coin and receipt cross four controls (interpretar, corrigir, estabilizar, julgar) and arrive, identical, at the dispositivo; the coin stays in reserve. "Após o saneamento, nunca" goes: the body itself admits the art. 190 convention.
2. 01 Prestação: vias (merged with liquidação), perecimento (inside/outside the vínculo), indivisível (new: whole machine, divided product).
3. 02 Cumulação: three states of the dispositivo sheets (independent, sucessiva, subsidiária), then recurso (new: overlaid sheets).
4. 03 Interpretar: desistência vs renúncia, two layers (the autos lifted, the right struck).
5. 04 Dispositivo: shelf check (quantity, binding, missing slip), then tutela (new: one machine by pressure or seizure, money only behind a gate).
6. 05 Controle: precise note vs sweeping note, fifteen working days in weeks, partial vs total withdrawal.
7. 06 Estabilidade: contraditório strips (new), then discovery as an exploded dossier (three states).

**Figures.** Stay and are redrawn (words-off passes): hero, vias, perecimento, cumulo, leitura, dispositivo, controle, discovery. Added: indivisível, recurso, tutela, estabilidade. Cut: fecho (a second copy of the hero), the fourth cumulo state (partial victory, which stays in the prose of state 3), the aposentadoria sheet inside leitura. Twelve figures, the same density as Aula 01.

**Text.** No fact added or dropped; quotes untouched. Moves: Faustina's case stays in prose; the indivisibility paragraph gets its own heading and figure; the "sucessiva" and recurso paragraphs sit beside the figures that carry them; discovery paragraphs become steps.
